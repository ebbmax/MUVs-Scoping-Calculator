⚠️ Additional Considerations
  • D360 Batch & Streaming Consumption: While Real-Time Personalization - MUVs (100K) covers D360 Realtime and Personalization consumption, customers must own/purchase D360 SKUs to cover batch processing. D360 capabilities that are not covered by MUVs include Batch/Rapid Segments, Batch/Streaming CIs, Batch Ingestion, Kinesis/Kafka streaming, Batch IR, and Activations. Customers can leverage Data Service Credits, Flex Credits, or D360 Profiles to cover consumption related to these capabilities.
  • Marketing Cloud Personalization Customers: For MCP customers looking to leverage the MUVs model to access Salesforce Personalization, they will need to own BOTH MCP+ and MUVs. MCP related consumption for unique visitors and named profiles can be dropped to $0.
  • Real-Time Profile Data Graphs: Monthly Unique individuals are not deduped across profile data graphs. More RT DGs can lead to increased MUV consumption.
